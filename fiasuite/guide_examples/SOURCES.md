# Sources of the guide example pictures

The pictures in this folder are made by `tools/make_guide_examples.py` from the photographs below, which are in the
public domain or released under CC0 on Wikimedia Commons. All forgeries in the examples were made by that script.

- **Dokkadeltaet nord i Randsfjorden.jpg** - CC0, by Øyvind Holmstad. Used as: base picture of most examples (half-size crop).  
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dokkadeltaet_nord_i_Randsfjorden.jpg
- **Bangkok fruit stall.jpg** - Public domain, by Michael. Used as: donor of the pasted objects (fruit only, half-size crop).  
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bangkok_fruit_stall.jpg
- **Wood St Galleries west jeh.jpg** - CC0, by Jim.henderson. Used as: geometry example.  
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wood_St_Galleries_west_jeh.jpg
- **SDXL P3-3.png** - Public domain, by SDXL using Fooocus. Used as: AI-detector and spectrum examples.  
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SDXL_P3-3.png
